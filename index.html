The weekly AI content workflow每周AI内容工作流程

The repeatable routine participants take home from the closing session — times are typical for a small team.学员在结业环节带走的可重复流程——所示时间为小团队的一般情况。

15 minPlan: AI suggests this week's themes from your content pillars.规划：AI根据内容支柱建议本周主题。
20 minWrite: captions, hooks and hashtags for every post.撰写：每则贴文的文案、开场钩子与标签。
20 minDesign: AI visuals and poster concepts in brand style.设计：符合品牌风格的AI视觉与海报概念。
15 minVideo: a short-form script and scene list, ready to film.视频：短视频脚本与分镜，随时开拍。
10 minReview: a person checks facts, tone and brand — then schedules.审核：由人检查事实、语气与品牌，再安排发布。
AI drafts — people decide. Every workflow includes a human review for accuracy, brand fit, cultural sensitivity and regulatory rules in your industry before anything is published. Free tiers of most tools are enough for class; some image and video tools have usage limits or paid plans, and we advise on options beforehand.AI负责起草，人负责决定。每个流程在发布前都设有人工审核，检查准确性、品牌契合度、文化敏感度及行业法规。课堂上大多数工具的免费版本已足够使用；部分图像与视频工具有使用限制或需付费，课前我们会提供建议。
